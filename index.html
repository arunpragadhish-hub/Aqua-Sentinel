The concept

One floating platform.
Multiple ways to help.

Water bodies have different needs. Aqua Sentinel is being designed as a modular concept that can be adapted and evaluated for different environments.

☀️

Solar energy

A renewable-energy approach intended to support system operation and reduce reliance on grid power where suitable.

💧

Multi-stage treatment

A proposed combination of screening, filtration and UV treatment modules, selected according to the water and intended use.

📡

Water-quality monitoring

Sensor and dashboard concepts can help operators observe selected water-quality indicators and follow changes over time.

🧩

Modular by design

A flexible concept intended for assessment in ponds, lakes, reservoirs and other suitable water bodies.

🌱

Environmental focus

Built around the goal of supporting better water stewardship and more informed environmental decisions.

🤝

Built for collaboration

We want to work with municipalities, site operators, researchers and environmental groups to validate real-world needs.

From concept to impact

How Aqua Sentinel is intended to work

Each deployment should be tailored to the water body, local conditions and the intended outcome.

STEP 01

Assess the water body

Understand site conditions, water-quality concerns, operating requirements and the right indicators to monitor.

STEP 02

Configure the modules

Explore suitable solar, sensor, intake and treatment modules with technical partners and site stakeholders.

STEP 03

Test and learn

Run a supervised pilot, measure results and refine the design before considering broader deployment.
